🎯 Ticket Triage & Routing

Automatically classify incoming support tickets by urgency and category, then route them to the right team queue.
1

Ticket Arrives in Zendesk

Customer submits support request through ShipStation help widget or email.

2

AI Classifies the Ticket

Claude API analyzes ticket text to determine:

  • Urgency: Critical, High, Medium, Low
  • Category: Shipping, Billing, Integration, Account, General
  • Sentiment: Frustrated, Neutral, Positive
  • Required Expertise: Technical, Billing, Operations
3

Auto-Route to Queue

Ticket is tagged and routed to the appropriate team:

URGENT + Shipping Issue → CRITICAL_SHIPPING queue High + Billing → BILLING queue Low + General → GENERAL_QUEUE
4

Agent Receives Enriched Ticket

Agent sees tags, urgency level, and suggested response template ready for review.

📋 Triage Categories for ShipStation

Shipping Issues: Tracking problems, delayed shipments, carrier issues
Billing & Invoicing: Refund requests, payment failures, subscription questions
Integration Help: API questions, webhook issues, third-party integrations
Account Management: Password resets, permission issues, user access
Technical/Troubleshooting: System errors, feature bugs, performance issues

📥 Customer writes: "My shipment hasn't updated in 5 days and customer is upset. What's going on?"

✍️ Response Drafting & QA

AI generates first-draft responses based on ticket content and knowledge base. Agents review, customize, and send.
1

Agent Opens Triaged Ticket

Ticket has urgency, category, and customer context ready to read.

2

AI Retrieves Relevant KB Articles

System searches knowledge base and returns top 3 matching articles:

[1] "How to track your ShipStation order" (92% match)
[2] "Why shipments are delayed" (87% match)
[3] "Carrier updates guide" (81% match)
3

AI Drafts Response

Claude generates a first-draft response that:

  • Acknowledges customer concern
  • Provides relevant KB info (with links)
  • Offers next steps
  • Uses professional but friendly tone
4

Agent Reviews & Customizes

Agent reads draft, adds personal touches, and either sends or revises before sending.

5

Response Sent

Agent marks ticket as resolved or sets follow-up.

📝 Example Draft Response

Customer Ticket: "My order hasn't updated in 5 days. I shipped it via FedEx last week. What's happening?"
AI-Generated Draft:
Hi [Customer Name],

Thanks for reaching out! I understand how frustrating delayed tracking updates can be.

Here's what's typically happening: FedEx sometimes has a 24-48 hour delay syncing tracking data with ShipStation. Here's what you can do:

1. Check FedEx directly: Visit fedex.com and enter your tracking number
2. Refresh in ShipStation: Click the refresh button on the shipment details
3. Check carrier settings: Ensure your FedEx API credentials are current
See our guide: [Link to "Why shipments are delayed"]
If the issue persists beyond 48 hours, reply with your shipment ID and I'll investigate further.

Best,
[Agent Name]
Team ShipStation Support

--- Agent Review Notes: ✓ Looks good! Added specific shipment ID for follow-up.

🎓 Response Quality Guidelines

✓ Do: Use first drafts as time-savers, add personal touches, include specific tracking numbers, offer next steps
✗ Don't: Send AI drafts without review, ignore KB links, make promises you can't keep, assume customer context

Scraped ShipStation docs + your internal KB are searchable and automatically embedded in AI responses.

Track KB Usage

Monitor which articles are most useful in responses. Update/improve low-performing docs.

❓ Common Questions

Q: Will AI replace my support team?
A: No. AI automates the slow parts (searching KB, writing drafts). Agents still review everything and maintain customer relationships.
Q: How do we handle edge cases?
A: If AI gets a ticket classification wrong, agent corrects it. These corrections train the model over time.
Q: What about sensitive data?
A: Customers' personal data stays in Zendesk. Only ticket subject/body goes to Claude API. Use your own VPC if needed.
Q: Can we use Jira Service instead of Zendesk?
A: Yes. Same workflow, different API. Setup is nearly identical.

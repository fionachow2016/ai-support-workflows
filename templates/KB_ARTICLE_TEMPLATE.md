## Template Tips

### Good Titles (Make It Clear)
✅ "How to Track Your Shipment"  
✅ "Fixing Carrier Sync Issues"  
✅ "Bulk Label Printing Guide"  
❌ "Tracking"  
❌ "System Setup"  
❌ "FAQ"

### Good Structure
✅ Quick answer first (TLDR for busy people)  
✅ Step-by-step (numbered, visual)  
✅ Screenshots (show exactly where to click)  
✅ Troubleshooting (common mistakes)  
✅ When to escalate (know your limits)  
❌ Long paragraphs (boring, hard to scan)  
❌ Technical jargon (confuses non-technical users)  
❌ No screenshots (vague instructions)

### Good Writing
✅ Use customer's language ("track my order" not "retrieve shipment metadata")  
✅ Be specific (steps with exact menu paths)  
✅ Keep it short (under 500 words if possible)  
✅ Use formatting (bold, bullets, numbered lists)  
✅ Test before publishing (follow your own instructions)

## Examples by Category

### Shipping Article Example
```
# How to Track Your Order

Quick Answer: Open Orders → Select shipment → Click "View Tracking"

When to Use This: Customer can't find tracking info

Steps:
1. Log into ShipStation
2. Go to Orders
3. Find your order
4. Click "View Tracking"
5. You'll see tracking number and carrier

Common Mistake: Looking in wrong section
Fix: Make sure you're in Orders, not Shipments

Escalate if: They can't see order at all
```

### Billing Article Example
```
# How to Request a Refund

Quick Answer: Go to Account → Billing → Request Refund

When to Use This: Customer wants to refund unused credits

Steps:
1. Log in to your account
2. Click Account (top right)
3. Select Billing
4. Find unused credits
5. Click "Request Refund"
6. Explain reason (required)
7. Submit

Timeline: 5-10 business days to process

Escalate if: More than $500 or disputes
```

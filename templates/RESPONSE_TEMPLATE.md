# Response Template

Use this template as a starting point for crafting responses in the AI-generated drafts.

---

## Standard Response Template

```
Hi [Customer Name],

[ACKNOWLEDGMENT - Validate their concern]
Thank you for reaching out about [their issue]. I completely understand your frustration with [specific context].

[KEY INFORMATION - Answer their question]
Here's what's happening and how to solve it:

1. [First step or explanation]
   [Include KB link: [Article Title](https://link)]

2. [Second step]
   [Include KB link if relevant]

3. [Third step]

[NEXT STEPS - What if this doesn't work?]
If this doesn't resolve your issue, please reply and let me know. I can [escalation option here].

[TIMELINE - Set expectations]
You'll hear back from us by [timeframe].

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Your Title]
ShipStation Support Team
```

[Detailed instructions]

[ESCALATION PATH]

1. Reply with [what to include]

- Error message (screenshot)

[Your Name]
[Your Title]
ShipStation Support Team

## Tips for Using These Templates

1. **Personalize** — Replace brackets with real customer info
2. **Shorten** — These are longer; cut unnecessary sections for simple questions
3. **Link articles** — Always include relevant KB links
4. **Match tone** — Adjust formality based on customer's tone
5. **Be specific** — Use customer's account details, order numbers, etc.
6. **Set expectations** — Always tell them when they'll hear back

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Generic greeting ("Hi there" instead of using their name)  
❌ No KB links (forcing them to search)  
❌ Vague next steps ("contact us if you need help")  
❌ No timeline ("we'll get back to you soon" is too vague)  
❌ Lengthy explanations (keep it under 3 paragraphs)  
❌ No signature (always sign with your name and title)

---

## Quick Reference

- **Response length**: 200-400 words (most cases)
- **Structure**: Acknowledge → Explain → Steps → Next Steps
- **Tone**: Professional but friendly
- **Links**: Include 1-3 KB articles maximum
- **Timeline**: Always specify (e.g., "by 3pm today", "within 2 hours")
